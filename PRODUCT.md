# Titan v1 — Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Titan Executive

---

# Vision

Every leader should begin their day knowing exactly what matters most.

Titan transforms fragmented organizational information into clear, trusted, and actionable executive intelligence.

---

# Problem

Organizations have more information than ever before.

That information is spread across dozens of different systems:

- Email
- Chat
- Finance
- Security
- CRM
- HR
- Documents
- Project Management

Leaders spend hours collecting information before they can even begin making decisions.

Important relationships between departments are often missed.

The problem is not a lack of data.

The problem is a lack of organizational understanding.

---

# Solution

Titan connects to an organization's existing software, continuously understands what is happening across the business, and delivers a prioritized executive briefing with recommendations supported by evidence.

Titan does not replace existing software.

Titan becomes the intelligence layer above it.

---

# Target Customer

Version 1 focuses on:

- CEOs
- Founders
- COOs
- Executive Directors

Organizations between:

10–250 employees

---

# Core User Story

As a CEO,

I want to log into Titan every morning,

So I immediately understand the most important issues affecting my organization and what decisions require my attention.

---

# Version 1 Features

## Organization

Create an organization.

---

## Dashboard

One executive dashboard.

---

## Daily Briefing

Every morning Titan provides:

- Top priorities
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Recommended actions

---

## Decision Tracking

Record decisions.

Track outcomes.

Learn over time.

---

## Organizational Memory

Remember:

- important events
- previous decisions

---

# Version 1 Does NOT Include

- AI employees
- Marketplace
- Mobile app
- Advanced automation
- Multi-organization management

---

# Success

A CEO opens Titan every morning because they trust it to answer one question:

"What do I need to focus on today?"
